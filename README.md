# Grocery Store Mobile App

### UX/UI Design Case Study

A mobile grocery shopping experience designed to improve the way customers browse products, understand product availability, discover coupons, and navigate their shopping experience.

---

## Project Overview

**Project Type:** Mobile App Design

**My Role:** Lead UX/UI Designer & UX Researcher

**Target Users:** Teens through adults of all genders in Florida

### Business Goal

The goal of the project was to elevate the overall grocery shopping experience through a more user-friendly mobile application.

The design focused on helping customers:

- Find products more efficiently
- Better understand product availability
- Discover coupons and savings
- Navigate the shopping experience more easily
- Personalize their experience

---

## The Problem

Customers can become frustrated when product availability is unclear or when finding products and savings requires too much effort.

The project explored how a mobile grocery application could make shopping more transparent, efficient, and convenient.

---

## Research

### Stakeholder Interview

The project began by gathering information through a stakeholder interview to better understand the business and customer needs.

The research helped establish the areas of the shopping experience that needed improvement.

---

## Understanding the User

### User Personas

Personas were used to represent different user needs and behaviors within the grocery shopping experience.

### Empathy Map

The empathy map helped explore what users may think, feel, say, and do throughout their shopping experience.

### User Flow

User flows were used to map how customers would move through the application and complete key shopping tasks.

---

## Competitive Audit

A competitive audit was used to evaluate existing grocery shopping experiences and identify opportunities for improvement.

The audit helped inform decisions around navigation, product discovery, shopping efficiency, and overall usability.

---

## Starting the Design

### Low-Fidelity Wireframes

I created low-fidelity wireframes to explore the structure and layout of the application before moving into more refined designs.

The wireframes focused on creating a clear shopping experience and making important features easier to access.

---

## Visual Design

### Typography

Typography was considered as part of creating a clear and consistent interface.

### Grid System

A grid system was used to create structure and consistency across the application's layouts.

### Mobile & Desktop Layouts

The layouts explored how the experience could adapt across different screen sizes while maintaining usability and visual consistency.

---

## A/B Testing

A/B testing was used to compare design approaches and evaluate which option provided a stronger experience for users.

The testing helped inform design decisions and refine the interface.

---

## Design Focus

Throughout the project, I focused on:

- Product availability transparency
- Efficient navigation
- Product discovery
- Coupon discovery
- Personalization
- Overall shopping efficiency
- User-friendly mobile interaction

---

## Final Design

The resulting design brings together the research, user flows, wireframes, visual system, and testing into a more streamlined grocery shopping experience.

The goal was to reduce friction and make it easier for customers to find what they need while discovering additional shopping opportunities.

---

## What I Learned

This project reinforced the importance of understanding both **user needs and business goals** when designing a digital product.

Research, competitive analysis, user flows, wireframing, and testing provided opportunities to evaluate different solutions before refining the interface.
